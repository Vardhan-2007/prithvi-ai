# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn and build, whatever your background or experience.

**Expected behaviour**
* Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
* Assume good intent, ask questions, and help newcomers.
* Give credit, including for data sources and AI-assisted work.
* Be honest about what the software can and cannot do.

**Not acceptable**
* Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome advances.
* Publishing others' private information.
* Misrepresenting simulated results as real forecasts.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may remove people who do not follow these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.

# 4-minute demo script (matches the project pack)

1. **0:00 Problem** — fragmented local risk information; we let people *explore* assumptions.
2. **0:30 Map** — open the 3D twin; point at buildings/roads (OSM), proxy inlets (cyan), facilities. Open **Data & limits**: every layer has source, licence, resolution, status.
3. **1:10 Baseline** — switch heat-map to *Risk index*, click a zone → **Zone** tab: four weighted factors, sentences, baseline marker.
4. **1:50 What-if** — preset *Heavy + 25 % blocked*, press play: rain, water appears, buildings/roads turn amber/red, hotspot depth pins. Drag the timeline. Click a cyan inlet to block it; watch nearby zones change. Switch on *Δ vs baseline* and the **Compare** view.
5. **2:50 Decision support** — **Overview** (flagged zones, roads km, facilities), **Priority**, then **Routes** (hospital → fire station, dry vs scenario).
6. **3:30 Trust** — **Assistant** answers with citations and refuses off-topic questions; **Data & limits**; say plainly: indicative scenario, not a forecast.

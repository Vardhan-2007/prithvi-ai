PRITHVI AI is not an official source. For real weather warnings use the India Meteorological Department at mausam.imd.gov.in. For national disaster-management guidance use the National Disaster Management Authority at ndma.gov.in. For river and reservoir information use the Central Water Commission at cwc.gov.in. For local alerts, shelters and emergency instructions follow your district administration and state disaster management authority.

In an emergency contact the official emergency services. Do not rely on this prototype for decisions about safety, travel or evacuation.

This is a short demo summary of commonly published flood-preparedness practice. It is not an official document. Verify against current guidance from your disaster management authority before relying on it.

Before heavy rain: know your local shelter locations and the official alert channels. Keep a small emergency kit with drinking water, medicines, a torch, a charged phone and important documents in a waterproof bag. Move valuables and electrical items above ground level where possible.

During flooding: follow instructions from local authorities. Avoid walking or driving through floodwater because depth, currents and hidden hazards are hard to judge. Keep away from fallen power lines and switch off electricity at the mains only if it is safe to reach the switch.

After flooding: avoid contact with floodwater, which can be contaminated. Do not return to flooded buildings until authorities say it is safe.

No surveyed drainage inventory was available for the pilot area. The storm-drain inlets shown in the twin are proxy points spread evenly along drivable roads. They do not show where real drains are.

The blockage slider marks a chosen share of those proxy inlets as unavailable, always the same inlets in the same order so results are repeatable. You can also click an individual inlet in the 3D view to block or clear it.

Blocking inlets raises the drainage vulnerability factor of nearby zones and removes drainage capacity from the water simulation. This is a sensitivity assumption. It is not an inspection record and does not claim that any real drain is blocked.

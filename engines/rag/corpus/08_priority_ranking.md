The priority list ranks zones by the scenario risk index weighted by exposure, with a small bonus for zones containing mapped critical facilities. The result is a suggestion for where planners might look first, for example to inspect drainage or pre-position resources.

It is not an instruction to evacuate, a dispatch order or a statement that any location is safe. Each entry shows the main driver of its score so that a person can judge whether the reasoning makes sense.
